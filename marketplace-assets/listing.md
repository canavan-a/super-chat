## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Super Chat is a private messenger where everything is end-to-end encrypted and nobody, including us, can read your messages.

No sign-up, no phone number, no email. Your account is a key that lives on your device, backed up by a 24-word recovery phrase that only you hold.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• End-to-end encryption for every message, file, picture and voice call
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• You appear under a different random ID in every room, so rooms can't be linked to each other
• Our server only ever stores encrypted data it has no key for
• Unlock with your fingerprint; screenshots are blocked

CHAT
• Group rooms and one-to-one chats
• Share photos and files, all encrypted
• Reactions, replies and read receipts
• Invite people with a link
• Choose how long messages are kept, or wipe a room's history

VOICE ROOMS
• Every room has a voice room for up to 8 people
• Drop in and out whenever you like
• Mute, sound off, and speaker or earpiece controls
• Calls are encrypted end to end
• Bring your own STUN/TURN servers, or share one with your room in a tap

YOURS TO KEEP
• Restore your account on a new device with your recovery phrase
• Several themes, including dark, slate, sepia and ember
• Open source and self-hostable: run your own server if you prefer

Super Chat collects no personal information. Read our privacy policy for exactly what our server stores (encrypted data only) and how long it is kept.
```
